What is a Graph?

A Graph is a non-linear data structure consisting of vertices (or nodes) and edges (the lines connecting the nodes). Unlike trees, graphs do not have a specific "root" and can contain cycles or loops.

Key Concept: Graphs are used to model real-world networks. They can be directed (one-way streets) or undirected (two-way streets), and edges can carry weights (distances or costs). They are the backbone of GPS routing, social network suggestions, and the internet itself.
